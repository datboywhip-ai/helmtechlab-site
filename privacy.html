Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers helmtechlab.com ("the site"), operated by Helm Tech Lab. It is short because the site collects very little.

What we collect

  • Contact form. If you use the form, we receive the name, email address and message you type. We use them to reply to you.
  • Email. If you email hello@helmtechlab.com, we receive your address and whatever you send.
  • Basic traffic data. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and keep it secure. If we turn on Cloudflare Web Analytics, it reports aggregate page views without cookies or cross-site tracking.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not run advertising trackers or set advertising cookies on this site.
  • We do not use what you send us to train AI models.

How long we keep it

Messages are kept for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the conversation. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Who handles it

Contact-form submissions are delivered through our hosting and email infrastructure (Cloudflare and our own systems). We share your information with others only if the law requires it or you ask us to.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@helmtechlab.com from the address you used and we will act on it.

Children

The site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
